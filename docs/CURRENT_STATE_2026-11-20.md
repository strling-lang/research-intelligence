Evidence IDs resolve in the [decision evidence register](DECISION_EVIDENCE_2026-11-20.md). `R` records the prior research baseline for every row. `S`, `W`, and `C` identify release/contract, website, and Conformance baselines. The maturity states refer to the entire inquiry. A bounded verified study can inform a decision while its enclosing inquiry still has unresolved questions.

**Reading dispositions.** Established downstream means a checked contract, result or implementation in the owning source. Independently related means the source resembles a research proposal and no documented adoption relationship was found. Verified research findings explicitly identify formal, source, or replay evidence. Provisional hypotheses need further verification. Rejected or superseded ideas retain their history. Unknown includes untested and unavailable evidence. Every row records `none` for formal downstream disposition as of this review. Accepted downstream requires an owner decision or controlling contract connecting an actual research recommendation to adoption. A product acceptance record establishes the scoped product decision.
