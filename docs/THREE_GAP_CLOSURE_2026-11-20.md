### Bounded compiler language analysis

The [implementation commit](https://github.com/strling-lang/strling/commit/40bc9e088600ae2663a1099a0fa6e5beab9bf569) adds a private `publish = false` command for `equivalent`, `contains`, and `disjoint`, consuming compiled or serialized canonical Semantic IR. Its case-sensitive scalar-domain projection admits exact literals, positive literal/range sets, concatenation, choice, and nonpossessive repetition under deterministic limits. Whole-input proofs close reachable pairs of ε-NFA state sets over an effective Unicode scalar partition. Refuted outcomes require independent membership replay of a shortest witness. Unsupported, malformed and exhausted inputs have distinct results. Canonical `empty` denotes the empty word. The accepted fragment has no direct node for the empty language.

The locked offline candidate suite passed six tests on Windows x64 and WSL2 Ubuntu x64 with Rust 1.75.0, covering 17 authored cases, 243 independent derivative comparisons and 360 direct finite memberships. Clippy and the focused frontend and kernel public-contract checks passed. A separate executable probe compared the choice `ß` or `🙂` with literal `ß`. It proved initially directed containment, returned `🙂` for equivalence and reversed containment refutations, and returned `ß` for disjointness refutation. The source record remains planned. Active compiler governance still applies the prior release task and rejects new tool paths under that task's scope. [Implementation and commands](https://github.com/strling-lang/strling/blob/40bc9e088600ae2663a1099a0fa6e5beab9bf569/core/tools/language-relations/README.md), [planned governance record](https://github.com/strling-lang/strling/blob/40bc9e088600ae2663a1099a0fa6e5beab9bf569/docs/migration/records/bounded-language-relations-review.yaml).
