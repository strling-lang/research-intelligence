## Bounded language analysis

The private, analysis-only tool decides whole-input equivalence, containment, and disjointness on a validated Semantic IR fragment. Its case-sensitive Unicode scalar projection admits literals, positive literal and range sets, sequence, choice, and nonpossessive repetition. The search closes reachable pairs of epsilon-closed NFA state sets over a finite effective alphabet. Refutations require independent witness replay. Budget exhaustion, unsupported constructs, and invalid inputs have distinct outcomes. Observable match selection, capture participation, and replacement behavior require separate proofs for any proposed rewrite. [Compiler PR #152](https://github.com/strling-lang/strling/pull/152), [merged model and commands](https://github.com/strling-lang/strling/blob/1cf698751e04d5b2130b0c2500358499657709fc/core/tools/language-relations/README.md).

Local locked Windows and WSL2 proof suites each passed all six tests, covering 17 authored cases, 243 independent derivative comparisons, 360 direct finite membership checks, and canonical digest verification across encodings. The two hosts emitted the same CLI reports for the supplementary-scalar witness, SHA-256 `95f9c13b378ee22bed31713fa18894c0892d3fca1fa4fd961f49c8476967c86d`. The final review preserved the adapter, sealed interop, and public kernel source bytes. Scope, hygiene, lock and API checks passed, along with warnings-denied Clippy in WSL2.

All three hosted development matrices passed on review head `d4d60979ce043f3a6f1d468625537d33d2ad1c3b`.
