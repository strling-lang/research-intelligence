# Regex Lab

## Purpose

Research a public interactive experience that may combine **Audit**, **Evaluate**, **Compare**, and **Playground** while preserving clear boundaries among static reasoning, controlled execution, compilation, and evidence.

## Important research questions

- Which user jobs and result contracts distinguish Audit, Evaluate, Compare, and Playground?
- How should exact engine, version, host, option, input, and uncertainty context appear?
- Which operations require controlled execution, compiler analysis, canonical knowledge, or public explanation?
- How should privacy, resource bounds, safety, reproducibility, and shareable artifacts work?

## Relationship to STRling

Regex Lab is a product research direction. The website owns public experience implementation; `strling` owns compiler behavior; Regex Knowledge and Regex Conformance own their source facts and observations.

## Current evidence boundary

Audit, Evaluate, Compare, Playground, and their umbrella are defined here as research/product concepts, not as claims of current public availability. Existing tooling reports cover embedded authoring assistance rather than a complete Lab product.

## Related existing inquiries

No dedicated Lab inquiry has begun. Migrated embedded-tooling and analysis inquiries may later supply bounded inputs.

## Future research opportunities

- user-job and interaction research;
- operation-specific result schemas;
- execution isolation and provenance;
- comparison and witness visualization; and
- handoff between imported regex and semantic STRling authoring.

## Explicit non-goals

This program does not implement the Lab, promise launch scope, run public untrusted regexes, or combine distinct authorities into one editable source of truth.
