# Beyond Regex

## Purpose

Investigate long-range capabilities adjacent to or beyond conventional regex: goal-directed evaluation, grammar systems, recursive patterns, non-text sequences, weighted and approximate matching, AI-assisted synthesis, neuro-symbolic concepts, and possible generalized pattern systems.

Inclusion in this program does not imply product commitment.

## Important research questions

- Which problems are meaningfully related to STRling's semantic compiler strengths, and which require separate systems?
- Where are boundaries among regular, parsing-expression, context-free, recursive, weighted, probabilistic, and general computation models?
- Which capabilities compose safely and intelligibly, and which create unacceptable semantic or operational coupling?
- What evidence would justify moving a question to a nearer horizon?

## Relationship to STRling

Research may reveal future opportunities or reasons not to expand. Any product or architecture decision belongs to `strling` and its owners. No virtual machine, grammar engine, probabilistic model, non-text system, or AI architecture is selected here.

## Current evidence boundary

The repository preserves broad historical reports as discovery inputs. Most were AI-assisted, mix distinct problem classes, and predate Fourth Edition. Their citations and conclusions require focused revalidation.

## Future research opportunities

Formal boundary maps, interoperable result and explanation models, comparisons against specialized systems, safety and verification boundaries, and criteria for product relevance.

## Explicit non-goals

This program is not the STRling roadmap, does not establish a Universal Pattern Engine, and does not imply that text, images, audio, tensors, biological data, and arbitrary patterns share one architecture.
