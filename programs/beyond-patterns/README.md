# Beyond Patterns

## Purpose

Investigate generalized symbolic pattern representation, relational pattern semantics, symbolic matching, bounded pattern discovery, evidence for discovered structure, and computational boundaries beyond conventional regex.

Beyond Patterns is a focused research program, not a STRling roadmap. Inclusion does not imply implementation, product commitment, or downstream acceptance. Shared vocabulary does not imply one intermediate representation or runtime, and domain-specific interpretation machinery may remain specialized.

## Relationship to Beyond Regex

[Beyond Regex](../beyond-regex/README.md) remains the broader umbrella for long-range regex-adjacent questions, including grammars, recursive systems, approximation, AI, goal-directed evaluation, non-text sequences, and generalized systems.

Beyond Patterns focuses on symbolic representations, relational pattern algebra, the symmetry and separation between matching and discovery, evidence-backed pattern induction, and the computational classifications those capabilities require. It does not replace or narrow Beyond Regex.

## Research boundary

A future architecture must be supported by convergence across focused inquiries, not repeated terminology or one synthesis proposal. The current evidence may justify formalization and falsifiable prototypes; it does not select a product architecture, one IR, one execution engine, or one extension mechanism.

Fourth Edition work remains independent. Nothing in this program creates Fourth Edition tasks, changes the current STRling semantic compiler, or describes a later STRling release.

## Current inquiries

- [Symbolic attribute models](../../inquiries/symbolic-attribute-models-for-generalized-pattern-recognition/README.md) — observations, typed projections and claims, context, grouping, ambiguity, and provenance.
- [Symbolic equivalence and confusability](../../inquiries/symbolic-equivalence-confusability-and-human-interpretable-representation/README.md) — typed exact, non-equivalent, contextual, and uncertain span relations.

- [Relational pattern algebra](../../inquiries/relational-pattern-algebra-for-sequences-and-structured-values/README.md) — structure, typed relations, views, captures, correspondence, and expressiveness limits.
- [Weighted symbolic similarity](../../inquiries/weighted-symbolic-similarity-and-evidence-combination/README.md) — typed evidence vectors, aggregation contracts, thresholds, and calibration boundaries.
- [Automatic discovery](../../inquiries/automatic-discovery-of-symbolic-patterns-in-data/README.md) — bounded hypothesis search, proposal portfolios, residual iteration, and independent replay.
- [Meaningfulness and false discovery](../../inquiries/meaningfulness-compression-recurrence-and-false-discovery/README.md) — multidimensional evaluation of discovered candidates and claim limits.
- [Computational boundaries](../../inquiries/computational-models-and-complexity-limits-of-symbolic-matching-and-discovery/README.md) — compiler-visible classes, tractability, budgets, dispatch, and refusal.
- [Symbolic pattern architecture synthesis](../../inquiries/symbolic-pattern-architecture-synthesis/README.md) — cross-report convergence, analogies, domain boundaries, conflicts, and the bounded shared-framework hypothesis.

## Important research questions

- What is the smallest formal contract that remains useful across genuinely different symbolic domains?
- Can specialized matchers and providers implement that contract without semantic loss or operational coupling?
- Which declarative fragment is expressive enough for matching and discovery output while remaining analyzable?
- What independence is required when an ordinary matcher verifies a discovered candidate?
- How should computational signatures, evidence profiles, and provenance compose without becoming universal scalar labels?
- What cross-domain task and user evidence would falsify or support a shared framework compared with interoperable specialist tools?

## Explicit non-goals

This program does not establish a Universal Pattern Engine, STRling 5.0, a generalized virtual machine, a universal feature ontology, a universal confidence score, or an implementation sequence. It does not absorb grammar, AI, approximation, or other Beyond Regex inquiries whose questions remain independent.
