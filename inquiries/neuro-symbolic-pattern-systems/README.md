# Neuro-symbolic pattern systems

## Research question

Do neuro-symbolic approaches provide capabilities beyond an ordinary separation of probabilistic candidate generation and deterministic verification for pattern systems?

## Motivation / decision informed

The answer could clarify whether a broader research program is justified or whether established compiler-and-tool boundaries are sufficient.

## Current state

Historical material uses “neuro-symbolic” for an architecture combining LLM generation, grammar constraints, compiler analysis, and repair. The same report is stored once under [AI-assisted pattern synthesis and verification](../ai-assisted-pattern-synthesis-and-verification/README.md); this inquiry isolates the more speculative scientific question.

## Scope

Learned and symbolic representations, bidirectional reasoning, proof or constraint integration, uncertainty, adaptation, interpretability, and evaluation criteria.

## Non-goals

This inquiry does not duplicate the report, select a neural architecture, claim that compiler feedback is inherently neuro-symbolic, or commit STRling to AI functionality.

## Research method

No focused research has been completed. Future work would require a primary-literature review, precise definitions, comparison against simpler baselines, and task-specific empirical evaluation.

## Evidence and source quality

Only a broad historical report currently motivates the inquiry. That is insufficient for an evidence-backed conclusion.

## Findings

No active finding is established beyond the need to distinguish ordinary tool orchestration from a substantive neuro-symbolic system.

## Limits, uncertainty, and negative knowledge

Relevant tasks, advantages, evaluation measures, data, models, and costs are unknown. The term itself is used inconsistently across literature and products.

## Alternatives considered

Deterministic compiler feedback around an ordinary generative model, symbolic search, constrained decoding, retrieval, program synthesis, and no AI component.

## Implications for STRling

None are established. The current compiler architecture does not require a neuro-symbolic interpretation.

## Recommendations

Keep Beyond Regex and defer until a concrete problem defeats simpler, auditable approaches.

## Rejected or abandoned alternatives

The assumption that combining an LLM and compiler automatically creates a committed neuro-symbolic STRling architecture is rejected.

## Downstream disposition

**Disposition:** none.
