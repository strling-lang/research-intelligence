# Inquiry agent guidance

An inquiry's `README.md` is the active synthesis for one durable question.
Reports under `reports/` are bounded, sourced inputs; they do not outrank their
sources or silently replace the synthesis. Small `artifacts/` may aid
reproducibility but are not product implementations, canonical knowledge, or
conformance evidence stores.

Before editing, read [README.md](README.md), the inquiry README, every report or
artifact directly relevant to the claim, and the framework documents linked
from the root `AGENTS.md`. Preserve one horizon, one honest maturity state,
concrete review triggers, limitations, uncertainty, negative knowledge, serious
alternatives, and traceable downstream disposition.

When new evidence materially changes a conclusion, explicitly preserve, amend,
supersede, invalidate, or leave it unresolved. Do not erase historical reports
or declare another repository's acceptance. Update the inquiry/program indexes
and bidirectional supersession links when applicable, then verify all local
links and material citations.
